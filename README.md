# Create-resume interaction prototype

Interactive prototype of the Kickresume onboarding start flow, built on the Kickresume design system.

1. **Intent screen** – "What do you want to get done first?" Four goals, all available, recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Intent screen [new]): **Build my resume**, **Tailor my resume**, **Pass the ATS check** and **Find jobs that fit me**. Each card's picture shows the outcome its text promises and is still at rest; on hover (or keyboard focus) its background deepens with a tint of the card's colour and one or two pieces ease into place: Build's AI line grows and its badge lifts, Tailor's job ad slides onto the resume and its keywords light up, the ATS flag turns into a tick and the score goes from 72 to 94, and the best job match rises out of the list. Build leads to the method screen; Tailor to the tailoring tool and the ATS check to Analyse resume, whose check ends in the ATS checker (without a resume both start on the method screen, which hands the resume straight on: Tailor to its setup and then the tailoring screen, the ATS check to Checking your resume and then the ATS checker); Find jobs to the job board (`#/jobs/search/`), or to Jobs for You when a Flow 1 run picked that variant (see Flow 1). Two layouts, switchable from the side rail: **Grid** (four cards in a 1048px row, two columns below ~1100px, rows with the picture on the left on phones) and **List** (500px cards stacked like the second screen).
2. **Creation screen** – "How do you want to create your resume?" ("We’ll fill in your details — you review and edit.") with four methods (Import from LinkedIn, Write with AI, Upload a resume, Paste text; white cards without an outline, as in Figma's Flow 1) and a link under them: **Back** when it returns to the goal screen (Flow 1) or the method screen, otherwise **Skip personalization** (to the dashboard, for someone who has signed up from a landing page or is in the app; the job board's link follows the same rule), in variants switchable from the side rail (A and B; C sits under the More options button, bottom right), plus a Flat / 3D look (also under More options):
   - **A · Expand in place** – all methods stay on one screen; clicking a card expands it (accordion, one open at a time); on shorter windows the other cards shrink to one line while one is open, in the same motion as the fold. On phones A uses the same two steps as B and C (list, then the method on its own screen with Back).
   - **B · Separate screens** – clicking a card slides to its own screen with a Back button; the other options are hidden. A quiet row of three short reassurances sits under the open card: how long it takes, the one risk people worry about for that method, and that everything stays editable. On phones they sit side by side as icon-over-label columns, each on one line (time shortened to "30 sec").
   - **C · Picture on top** (hidden for now: its More options button is gone and `#c` opens A; everything is still in the code, `SHOW_C` brings it back) – B's list and navigation; when a method opens, its title and subtitle cross-fade in as the page headline and the card folds away its own header (icon, title, subtitle), so the card opens straight onto a tall illustration in the style of the goal-screen cards, showing what you give and what you get (LinkedIn profile → resume, your answers → AI → resume, old PDF/DOC → fresh template, messy notes → sorted sections). The picture is 250px tall on desktop (180px on phones), with the drawing taking about 60% of its height so it doesn't dominate; on shorter laptop windows the page scrolls a little. Opening, going Back and switching methods each move the page in a single motion. Back restores the original headline. No reassurance row or extra help text. Errors stay inline with links to another method (Back always returns to the list). Prototype triggers: a LinkedIn link containing `private` fails the import; a file name containing `scan` can't be read.
   - **Look: Flat / 3D** (under More options, bottom right; works with A, B and C) – the 3D look gives the goal screen (grid and list) and the method screen a lighter, more modern surface built only from brand tokens: a faint dot grid behind most of the page that fades out gradually toward the edges (the resume fragments in the margins are hidden for now, see `CLAUDE.md`), frosted-glass cards with a hairline edge that takes the method's colour on hover (the open method is a plain white card), gradient icon tiles (the same light fill for every method, in its colour, no drop shadow), glass pills for Back and the chevrons, a frosted reassurance tray with a little more room under the card (B) and a faint glow under the call to action. Falls back to solid white surfaces when the system asks for reduced transparency. (This replaces the former variant D, which was B in this look.)

3. **Upload modal or building screen** – what follows the method screen depends on how the resume comes in.
   - **Uploaded or imported** (Upload a resume, Import from LinkedIn; also the Upload resume and LinkedIn import screens) – the **upload modal**, recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › the upload modals): it takes the methods' place under the method screen's headline (or the card's place on those screens) and goes through **Uploading resume... → Importing resume... → Finishing upload... → Upload successful!** (a LinkedIn profile starts at Importing resume..., with LinkedIn's 30-day note instead of the file-format tip). Its picture and the line under it are those of the feature the resume is for: Analytics (the ATS check), Tailor CV to Job, Resume Rewrite, Summary Generator, Interview Questions, AI Counsellor (Career coach) and Feedback (Recruiter feedback); anything else (Build my resume, the Upload resume screen, Proofreading, a cover letter) gets Resume Upload only's. Continue goes straight on to the destination or the next step: the editor, or the tool that asked for the resume. While it uploads there is no Continue; it shows once the upload is done. With the dock's Ends set to Failed or Error it ends in **Upload failed.** (Error: "The server is taking too long to respond."; Failed: the file or profile can't be read) with Back (to the methods or the screen's form) and Retry (runs it again after an error, back to the input after a failure).
   - **Created** (Write with AI, Paste text) – the **building screen**: the resume visibly takes shape step by step, then straight on to the destination or the next step in the same way.
4. **Editor** – the built page travels into the preview pane of the resume editor (sidebar, Fill In form or Design tab, live preview). **A new resume always opens on the Design tab** (from Figma: Templates, Format and Layout tabs; Templates has the All templates / ATS Friendly / Popular / Creative filters, the colours, which recolour the preview's accent, and the template grid with ATS and FREE badges; Format and Layout are wireframes until they have a design): whatever made it (the method screen's upload modal or building screen, New resume, a template or sample URL, Create Resume with AI, Upload resume, LinkedIn import, Flow 1's Build my resume, and their landing pages). Opening a resume that already exists (`/edit/`, the latest resume, the menu's Fill In), a section writer, a letter, a tailored or rewritten copy and the guest editor open Fill In; the menu's Design opens the Design tab. Editing fields updates the preview; clicking a preview section opens it in the form. Above the preview: **Edit Preview**, **Resume Tailoring** and **Help Center**. After a tailoring (Tailor resume's Done! → Continue, or a flow that ends in the tailored copy) the editor opens on **Resume Tailoring**, recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Initial screen - after tailoring completes, and the frames beside it): "This resume has been tailored" with the job it was tailored to (the job page's job opens that page; a link or a pasted post shows where it would open), the overall strategy, **What we changed** (9 changes, each opening to what changed and the keywords it added) and **Things to consider** (3 tips). Under the changes, the added keywords fold to two rows under View All Keywords; picking one marks and opens the changes that added it (picking it again lets go). Edit Preview brings the resume back. Before any tailoring, Resume Tailoring opens Tailor resume; a newly built resume, a run started from the dock or No CV clear it. The Fill In side and the rest of the editor are as before. On phones only Edit Preview and Resume Tailoring show, once there is a tailoring.
5. **The app menu** – every page inside the app (the editor, the dashboard, My Documents and the resume tools) has the same left menu as the editor: logo, account, Dashboard, My Documents, the current resume (Fill In, Design, ATS Checker, Proofread & Translate, Download & Share), Career Map, Job Interviews, Find Jobs, Job Tracker, and the plan with Upgrade; on phones it is the same top bar with a menu button. The current page is marked, and the links open their pages: Dashboard, My Documents, the editor (Fill In), its Design tab, the ATS checker, Proofreading, Career Map, Job Interview Questions, Find Jobs (Jobs for You), the Job Tracker (a wireframe) and the Premium page (Design and Download & Share say they aren't part of the prototype). The resume group shows on the resume pages, and on the dashboard and My Documents once there is a CV. Moving between these pages the menu stays in place.
   **Dashboard** – where people land if they skip personalization (“Skip personalization” on the goal screen). Its Job Openings list and logos follow Figma's New user dashboard. Its LinkedIn, PDF import and resume builder shortcuts drop you into the creation flow with that method already open.
6. **Route screens** – product screens recreated pixel for pixel from screenshots, each at its own URL and listed under More options › URLs (bottom right), in six groups by what the feature takes in and gives back: Get a resume, Improve my resume, Check my resume, Write something new, Find jobs and Just open (the features in one group ask for the same data, load the same way and end in the same kind of place; the dock's Open uses the same groups). They are full screens like the rest of the flow, not pop-ups: the same logo bar and background, content starting on the same line as the other screens' headlines, and the same slide transition in and out. Their cards use the same surface as the goal and method cards (thin grey outline, 16px corners); everything inside the card matches the screenshot, except that a setup card no longer has its own ×: their setup steps have an × in the screen's top right corner instead (32px from the edge, level with the logo, as on the template page; not while a screen processes, on a result or destination, on the jobs pages, on the job board (which has Back under it, as in the design) or on the sign-up wall). × and Cancel go back to where you came from, also when the method screen came in between, but someone who has signed up or logged in never goes back to Sign up, and in a run (not a guest's) not to the goal screen either: they land on the dashboard, and the flow bar says Closed → Dashboard (in Flow 1, Skipped personalization → Dashboard; a step in the bar takes them back into the run). When a screen or a step changes, keyboard focus moves to its headline, so Tab goes on from there and a screen reader says where you are; groups of choices (tone, form, seniority, the resume to use, the billing period) are one Tab stop with arrow keys.
   - **Sign up** (`#/register/`) and **Log in** (`#/login/`) – full pages as in the product, so unlike the other route screens they have their own top bar (logo, "Already have an account? / Don't have an account?" and Log In / Sign Up, which switch between the two) and a 400px photo column: a carousel of people hired with Kickresume Premium. Its six dots match the product; dots 1 and 2 switch between the two people the screenshots show, the other four wait for their photos. Signing up needs the consent box (without it the box turns coral and takes focus). Sign up leads to the goal screen and Log in to the dashboard; in a flow they move it on. The flows use these pages as their entry (Flow 1's runs, Flow 3's landing-page runs, and Flow 4's Log in and Translate resume); in Flow 3 the bottom note says the visitor's CV is attached, since the design doesn't show it. Below 900px the photo column hides; on phones only the Log In / Sign Up button stays in the top bar.
   - **Upgrade** (`#/upgrade/yearly|quarterly|monthly/`) and **Verify student status** (`#/upgrade/student/`) – recreated from the Figma files (Premium › A, Kickresume 3.0 › ISIC/ITIC). Like Sign up they are full pages of their own, on the Premium purple gradient, so the card keeps the design's drop shadow. The Premium page is the billing card (Premium = more jobs: perks and two hired people | Select billing period: Yearly, Quarterly marked Popular, Monthly, the money-back line and Upgrade Now) and the reviews panel under it (a quote, Trustpilot, Google and App Store ratings, and "Are you a student or a teacher? … for free today"). The URL's plan is picked when the page opens; picking another card (click or arrow keys) makes it the large one with the gradient border and changes the URL. "for free today" opens the student page: ISIC/ITIC tab (UniDays and Other say they aren't part of the prototype), full name, country, institution (a country's universities once a country is picked), card number, and Continue, which checks the fields and then says the card would be verified. × goes back where you came from (from the student page, to the plans if you came from there). Below 900px the billing card and the reviews panel stack; on phones the plan's payment terms go under the price and the student page drops the card picture.
   - **My Documents** (`#/dashboard/resumes/`) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Dashboard - My Documents). A full page with the app menu on the left (the design's own top bar is replaced by it); the panel is white on the app's grey. The Resumes tab shows the "create" card (New Resume and Create New → the method screen, New Resume with AI, Import from LinkedIn, Import PDF → their screens, Use Example says it isn't part of the prototype) and, with a CV, the resumes (the design's four, with pages 1–8): a resume opens the editor at `#/edit/`. Without a CV only the create card shows; with no flow running, switching the dock's CV redraws the page.
   - **Latest resume** (`#/latest-resume/…`) – with a CV, `#/latest-resume/` opens the editor at `#/edit/`, `…/analyze/` the ATS checker, `…/proofread/` Proofreading and `…/recruiter-feedback/` the Career Coach (Beta) page, which is the design given for recruiter feedback. Without a CV each of them leads to My Documents (`#/dashboard/resumes/`). The three tool pages are recreated from Figma (Analytics 4 PREVIEW, Download/share link active - update, AI Counsellor one expanded) as full pages with the app menu on the left (the designs' own top bars and menus are replaced by it) and the resume preview on the right; in the proofreading design the preview tabs are blurred, and so they are here. The ATS checks fold, the score rows jump to their card and the word-choice arrows change the category; Proofreading's Translate and Career Coach's Generate (which needs a job title) show that they're working and then a note; Upgrade opens the Premium page and × comes back. Under 1440px the preview narrows with its column, under about 1180px it hides, and on phones the content takes the full width under the app's top bar.
   - **Select your template** (hidden for now: `SHOW_TPL_PICKERS = false`; `#/dashboard/create-resume/` is New resume, the method screen, and every new document gets the default template; the cover letter editor also offers Write it with AI and From a sample, the website editor From my resume and Import from LinkedIn) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › 61941:33517). A full page of its own like Sign up: the logo (in the design's one grey) and × in its own top bar, a grey-to-white wash with the Kickresume symbol faint in it, "Select your template.", four filter chips (All templates, ATS Friendly, Popular, Creative; the picked one is dark, hover and keyboard focus darken a chip's outline) and the design's twelve templates (Dotts appears twice, as in the design; a filter shows each template once). A template lifts on hover (the design shows Doodle hovered) and opens the editor, or moves a running flow on to processing; × goes back to the screen you came from. On phones (the design's 375px frame) the logo is left out, the headline is smaller, the chips start at the left and the templates are in two columns.
   - **Create Resume with AI** (`#/dashboard/create-ai-resume/`) – Basic / Personalized tabs (Personalized adds the job-post field with a 0/6000 counter), name, job title, language, Generate with AI (purple gradient) → building screen → editor. Generate stays disabled until the name and job title are filled (in Personalized, the job post too).
   - **Generate work experience** (`#/ai/resume/` and `#/ai/resume/experience/`), **Generate resume objective** (`#/ai/resume/general/`) and **Summary generator** (`#/dashboard/summary-generator/`) – they need nothing first: Fill In opens at their section and the writer pops up over it (a dimmed editor; × , Escape or a click beside it closes it). Generate opens the **Job Interviews** page (Figma concepting-KR › 285:361, an app page with the app menu): first "Your interview questions are loading...", then the questions: the job title, Interview ⇄ Follow Up, the list (a question opens on the right with why it's asked, a model answer and why the answer works; View More shows the rest) and Hide Answer.

   - **Tailor resume** (`#/dashboard/tailor-resume/`) and **Tailor resume to a job** (from the job page's Tailor Your Resume, see below) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › tailoring setup and Tailoring new loading - update). Group 4 (Generate with AI) asks for one only in the writers' personalized step 2.
7. **Four flow templates** – every URL follows one of four flows (the flow board in Figma); the URL only fills in the feature's own screens (its personalization), the wording of the processing and the destination. The **run dock**, one row at the bottom right beside the More options button, works the way the app will: the URL says where the person ends up, and where they come from decides which of the four flows that makes. Left to right: **Open** (the URL, in the six groups; its list opens upwards and scrolls inside itself, with arrow keys, a letter, Enter and Escape), **From** (Sign up: a new user from /register, asked what they want first; App: the menu, the dashboard, an email or the URL itself; Landing page: a new user, with a CV and the feature's details given there or not; Guest: no account yet; choices a URL doesn't have are dimmed), **CV** (a switch, dimmed when where they come from decides it), **Data** (the landing page also gave the feature's details; dimmed where that can't happen), **Ends** (how the processing ends: Completed, Failed or Error) and **Run**, which names the flow that makes (Flow 1–4; hover for its name and number of steps) and starts it; it has a green ring while that run is going. So trying another flow is a click or two, with nothing to open first. Flow 1 is only `/register/` (the goal picked there is a branch of it); Tailor resume is Flow 2 from the App (with a CV), Flow 4 as a Guest, and from a Landing page Flow 3 with a CV (a landing page that takes the CV takes the job too, like Translate's takes the language), Flow 2 without one; Analyse resume and Translate resume from the App are Flow 4 (they need a CV, and the app shows them only with one). On phones the dock gets its own row above the version tag and More options, with short labels (Landing, Done, ▶ 3).
   - **Flow checker** (More options › Flows) – turn it on to go through the prototype as a new user: it starts on Sign up as Flow 1 (the goal you pick decides the rest), and once a run has reached its destination (or you skipped to the dashboard), whatever you open in the app (a tool from the menu, the dashboard, Find Jobs) becomes its own run, so the bar always says which flow and step you're in. Restart and reloading start on Sign up again while it's on; it's remembered. Off ends the run.
   - **The flow bar** – a bar at the bottom shows the run grouped by stage (hollow dots are wireframes); it sits just above the dock; ‹ › and the dots jump between steps, and the screens' own buttons move it on too. Restart, or opening a screen that isn't one of the run's steps (from URLs or by URL), ends it.
   - **How a URL picks its flow** (the doc "URLs by flow and situation") – the URL names the destination; the person sees only the steps for what they don't have yet, in this order: the goal (only `/register/`) → Sign up or Log in → a CV (method screen → upload modal or building screen; a CV from a landing page only goes through the upload modal) → the feature's data (its setup or form) → its loading → the destination. The flow number follows: **Flow 1** the goal screen was shown, **Flow 2** a CV step or a setup screen, **Flow 3** only loading (including the upload of a landing page's CV), **Flow 4** straight to the destination. Skip personalization (Flow 1) goes to the dashboard.
   - **One happy path per primary feature** – tailoring, resume building, translate, proofreading, the ATS check, finding, tracking and matching jobs, Career Map, the AI writer and the AI rewriter each follow one path, the same from a landing page and in the app (the app only skips what the person already has): one CV step (the method screen, then the upload modal or the building screen; Career Map uses its own Select a resume instead), always CV → data → loading (a landing page's CV is uploaded before the feature's form, and goes straight into the loading of a feature that has its own, with no upload modal: tailoring, rewriting, matching, the map; data from a landing page skips the form, never the CV), a loading screen only where something is generated, a new resume on Design, generated content on Fill In or Resume Tailoring, a check on its page, and Translate's language as a field on its page that a landing page fills in (from a landing page Translate ends in Fill In with the translated copy).
   - **The situations** – Open a URL in the dock, then From: **Landing page** (a new user; CV on = the landing page took a CV, Data on = it also took the feature's details: the job post, the letter's details, the role, the career answers, the language), **App** (logged in; the CV switch is whether they have one), **Sign up** (only `/register/`, Flow 1) or **Guest** (no account: they land straight in the app, Flow 4, with no resume creation or other screens first: the guest editor, Create Resume with AI and Tailor resume open the editor, the job board its page; the app menu, Download, Upgrade, AI and Resume Tailoring open a **sign-up pop-up** over the page, and Sign up or Log in brings them back to where they were). In the app (logged in) a run never goes through resume creation: the resume checks (Analyse resume, Recruiter feedback, Career coach, Proofreading, Translate) aren't shown without a CV, and Tailor resume, Rewrite resume, Jobs for You and the editor are shown but can't be reached without one, so none of them has an App run without a CV (their landing pages still have one). Landing pages collect a CV only for URLs that need one (not the resume-building ones: New resume, a template, Create Resume with AI are for someone without a resume, and a landing page's upload block leads to Upload resume); Log in, the editor (`/edit/` only opens with a document: an uploaded resume, a sample…; on its own it is a 404), the latest-resume links and the guest editor have no landing page. Translate's landing page always gives the language.
   - **Flow 1 · Goal first** – from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Flow 1): it starts at sign-up (`/register`), so there's no CV yet. Build (method screen → upload modal or building screen → editor), Tailor (method screen → … → Tailor resume → tailoring → the tailored copy), the ATS check (method screen → … → the ATS checker), and Find jobs two ways: the **Job board** or **Jobs for You** (method screen → … → Analyzing your resume… → your top matches). Picking another goal during the run switches the run to it.
   - **Flow 2 · something to collect** – a CV step and/or a setup screen: New resume and a template URL without a CV, Create Resume with AI, Upload resume, LinkedIn import, Tailor resume (setup → tailoring), Tailor resume to a job, Rewrite resume, Jobs for You and the Check-my-resume tools from a landing page without a CV, a landing page's CV with a setup still to fill (the setup first; the CV is uploaded with the processing after it, so the steps keep the template's order), the AI Cover Letter Writer, Job Interview Questions, Resignation letter and Career Map (their form → writing → the result).
   - **Flow 3 · only loading** – a landing page's CV is uploaded after sign-up and the run goes straight on (the ATS checker, Proofreading, Career Coach, the rewritten copy, Jobs for You's analysis, Translate with the language), Rewrite resume and Jobs for You with a CV, and the writers and tailoring when the landing page also gave their data. If a landing page's CV can't be uploaded, Back goes on as the feature's own run that asks for it again (the ATS check, tailoring) or to Upload your resume.
   - **Flow 4 · straight to the destination** – nothing missing: the section writers (Fill In + their pop-up), the check-my-resume pages and the editor with a CV, a sample or a cover letter or website URL, the job board, search results, Job, Company, Job Tracker, Log in, the dashboard, My Documents, the latest-resume links (My Documents without a CV) and the Upgrade pages.
   - **Processing and its result** – one processing screen for every feature (the upload modal or the building screen for resumes, Tailor resume's tailoring screen, a wireframe for the rest) that moves on by itself; the upload modal and the tailoring screen show their own result (Upload successful! or Upload failed., Done! or Something went wrong), and on the method screen the bar has a dot for each of the two ways (upload modal, building screen). Its result is Completed, Failed or Error (the dock's Ends): Completed goes straight on to the destination; Failed shows the processing state with a way back to change the input; Error lets the person try again. When it fails or errs, the building screen stops after its second step without "ready" or confetti. "Change the input" goes back to the input just given (the run's last personalization step); a Flow 3 run has none, since its input came before (a landing page's CV, a template or sample in the URL), so it goes on as the feature's own Flow 2 run that asks for it again (Upload your resume, the method screen, the template picker). The processing state wireframe can switch between the three too.
   - Results that would open a screen the prototype doesn't have (a cover letter, a resignation letter) show a short "Writing…" and then a note.
