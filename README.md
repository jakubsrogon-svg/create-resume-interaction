# Create-resume interaction prototype

Interactive prototype of the Kickresume onboarding start flow, built on the Kickresume design system.

1. **Intent screen** – "What do you want to get done first?" Four goals, all available, recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Intent screen [new]): **Build my resume**, **Tailor my resume**, **Pass the ATS check** and **Find jobs that fit me**. Each card's picture shows the outcome its text promises and is still at rest; on hover (or keyboard focus) its background deepens with a tint of the card's colour and one or two pieces ease into place: Build's AI line grows and its badge lifts, Tailor's job ad slides onto the resume and its keywords light up, the ATS flag turns into a tick and the score goes from 72 to 94, and the best job match rises out of the list. Build leads to the method screen; Tailor to the tailoring tool and the ATS check to Analyse resume, whose check ends in the ATS checker (without a resume both start on the method screen: Tailor then goes on to its setup, whose Tailor Now builds the resume and tailors it on the building screen, and the ATS check straight to its own building screen, Analyzing your resume, and then the ATS checker); Find jobs to the job matcher (`#/jobs/matcher/`, a wireframe). Two layouts, switchable from the side rail: **Grid** (four cards in a 1048px row, two columns below ~1100px, rows with the picture on the left on phones) and **List** (500px cards stacked like the second screen).
2. **Creation screen** – "How do you want to start?" with four methods (LinkedIn, AI, Upload, Paste text), in variants switchable from the side rail (A and B; C sits under the More options button, bottom right), plus a Flat / 3D look (also under More options):
   - **A · Expand in place** – all methods stay on one screen; clicking a card expands it (accordion, one open at a time); on shorter windows the other cards shrink to one line while one is open, in the same motion as the fold. On phones A uses the same two steps as B and C (list, then the method on its own screen with Back).
   - **B · Separate screens** – clicking a card slides to its own screen with a Back button; the other options are hidden. A quiet row of three short reassurances sits under the open card: how long it takes, the one risk people worry about for that method, and that everything stays editable. On phones they sit side by side as icon-over-label columns, each on one line (time shortened to "30 sec").
   - **C · Picture on top** – B's list and navigation; when a method opens, its title and subtitle cross-fade in as the page headline and the card folds away its own header (icon, title, subtitle), so the card opens straight onto a tall illustration in the style of the goal-screen cards, showing what you give and what you get (LinkedIn profile → resume, your answers → AI → resume, old PDF/DOC → fresh template, messy notes → sorted sections). The picture is 250px tall on desktop (180px on phones), with the drawing taking about 60% of its height so it doesn't dominate; on shorter laptop windows the page scrolls a little. Opening, going Back and switching methods each move the page in a single motion. Back restores the original headline. No reassurance row or extra help text. Errors stay inline with links to another method (Back always returns to the list). Prototype triggers: a LinkedIn link containing `private` fails the import; a file name containing `scan` can't be read.
   - **Look: Flat / 3D** (under More options, bottom right; works with A, B and C) – the 3D look gives the goal screen (grid and list) and the method screen a lighter, more modern surface built only from brand tokens: a faint dot grid behind most of the page that fades out gradually toward the edges (the resume fragments in the margins are hidden for now, see `CLAUDE.md`), frosted-glass cards with a hairline edge that takes the method's colour on hover (the open method is a plain white card), gradient icon tiles (the same light fill for every method, in its colour, no drop shadow), glass pills for Back and the chevrons, a frosted reassurance tray with a little more room under the card (B) and a faint glow under the call to action. Falls back to solid white surfaces when the system asks for reduced transparency. (This replaces the former variant D, which was B in this look.)

3. **Building screen** – after you submit a method, the resume visibly takes shape step by step.
4. **Editor** – the built page travels into the preview pane of the resume editor (sidebar, Fill In form, live preview). Editing fields updates the preview; clicking a preview section opens it in the form.
5. **The app menu** – every page inside the app (the editor, the dashboard, My Documents and the resume tools) has the same left menu as the editor: logo, account, Dashboard, My Documents, the current resume (Fill In, Design, ATS Checker, Proofread & Translate, Download & Share), Career Map, Job Interviews, Find Jobs, Job Tracker, and the plan with Upgrade; on phones it is the same top bar with a menu button. The current page is marked, and the links open their pages: Dashboard, My Documents, the editor (Fill In), the ATS checker, Proofreading, Career Map, Job Interview Questions, the jobs search and the Job Tracker (wireframes) and the Premium page (Design and Download & Share say they aren't part of the prototype). The resume group shows on the resume pages, and on the dashboard and My Documents once there is a CV. Moving between these pages the menu stays in place.
   **Dashboard** – where people land if they skip personalization (“Skip personalization” on the goal and method screens). Its LinkedIn, PDF import and resume builder shortcuts drop you into the creation flow with that method already open.
6. **Route screens** – product screens recreated pixel for pixel from screenshots, each at its own URL and listed under More options › URLs (bottom right), grouped like the URL checklist (1 Sign up / log in, 2 Guest, 3 Start from a template, 4 Generate with AI, 5 Bring a resume to a tool, 6 Latest resume, 7 Jobs, 8 Career planning, 9 Upgrade). They are full screens like the rest of the flow, not pop-ups: the same logo bar and background, content starting on the same line as the other screens' headlines, and the same slide transition in and out. Their cards use the same surface as the goal and method cards (thin grey outline, 16px corners); everything inside the card matches the screenshot, except that a setup card no longer has its own ×: their setup steps have an × in the screen's top right corner instead (32px from the edge, level with the logo, as on the template page; not while a screen processes, on a result or destination, on the jobs pages other than the job matcher, or on the sign-up wall). × and Cancel go back to where you came from, also when the method screen and the building screen came in between. When a screen or a step changes, keyboard focus moves to its headline, so Tab goes on from there and a screen reader says where you are; groups of choices (tone, form, seniority, the resume to use, the billing period) are one Tab stop with arrow keys.
   - **Sign up** (`#/register/`) and **Log in** (`#/login/`) – full pages as in the product, so unlike the other route screens they have their own top bar (logo, "Already have an account? / Don't have an account?" and Log In / Sign Up, which switch between the two) and a 400px photo column: a carousel of people hired with Kickresume Premium. Its six dots match the product; dots 1 and 2 switch between the two people the screenshots show, the other four wait for their photos. Signing up needs the consent box (without it the box turns coral and takes focus). Sign up leads to the goal screen and Log in to the dashboard; in a flow they move it on. The flows use these pages as their entry (Flow 1's runs, Flow 3's landing-page runs, and Flow 4's Log in and Translate resume); in Flow 3 the bottom note says the visitor's CV is attached, since the design doesn't show it. Below 900px the photo column hides; on phones only the Log In / Sign Up button stays in the top bar.
   - **Upgrade** (`#/upgrade/yearly|quarterly|monthly/`) and **Verify student status** (`#/upgrade/student/`) – recreated from the Figma files (Premium › A, Kickresume 3.0 › ISIC/ITIC). Like Sign up they are full pages of their own, on the Premium purple gradient, so the card keeps the design's drop shadow. The Premium page is the billing card (Premium = more jobs: perks and two hired people | Select billing period: Yearly, Quarterly marked Popular, Monthly, the money-back line and Upgrade Now) and the reviews panel under it (a quote, Trustpilot, Google and App Store ratings, and "Are you a student or a teacher? … for free today"). The URL's plan is picked when the page opens; picking another card (click or arrow keys) makes it the large one with the gradient border and changes the URL. "for free today" opens the student page: ISIC/ITIC tab (UniDays and Other say they aren't part of the prototype), full name, country, institution (a country's universities once a country is picked), card number, and Continue, which checks the fields and then says the card would be verified. × goes back where you came from (from the student page, to the plans if you came from there). Below 900px the billing card and the reviews panel stack; on phones the plan's payment terms go under the price and the student page drops the card picture.
   - **My Documents** (`#/dashboard/resumes/`) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › Dashboard - My Documents). A full page with the app menu on the left (the design's own top bar is replaced by it); the panel is white on the app's grey. The Resumes tab shows the "create" card (New Resume and Create New → the method screen, New Resume with AI, Import from LinkedIn, Import PDF → their screens, Use Example says it isn't part of the prototype) and, with a CV, the resumes (the design's four, with pages 1–8): a resume opens the editor at `#/edit/`. Without a CV only the create card shows; switching the CV in More options › URLs redraws the page.
   - **Latest resume** (`#/latest-resume/…`) – with a CV, `#/latest-resume/` opens the editor at `#/edit/`, `…/analyze/` the ATS checker, `…/proofread/` Proofreading and `…/recruiter-feedback/` the Career Coach (Beta) page, which is the design given for recruiter feedback. Without a CV each of them leads to My Documents (`#/dashboard/resumes/`). The three tool pages are recreated from Figma (Analytics 4 PREVIEW, Download/share link active - update, AI Counsellor one expanded) as full pages with the app menu on the left (the designs' own top bars and menus are replaced by it) and the resume preview on the right; in the proofreading design the preview tabs are blurred, and so they are here. The ATS checks fold, the score rows jump to their card and the word-choice arrows change the category; Proofreading's Translate and Career Coach's Generate (which needs a job title) show that they're working and then a note; Upgrade opens the Premium page and × comes back. Under 1440px the preview narrows with its column, under about 1180px it hides, and on phones the content takes the full width under the app's top bar.
   - **Select your template** (`#/dashboard/create-resume/`) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › 61941:33517). A full page of its own like Sign up: the logo (in the design's one grey) and × in its own top bar, a grey-to-white wash with the Kickresume symbol faint in it, "Select your template.", four filter chips (All templates, ATS Friendly, Popular, Creative; the picked one is dark, hover and keyboard focus darken a chip's outline) and the design's twelve templates (Dotts appears twice, as in the design; a filter shows each template once). A template lifts on hover (the design shows Doodle hovered) and opens the editor, or moves a running flow on to processing; × goes back to the screen you came from. On phones (the design's 375px frame) the logo is left out, the headline is smaller, the chips start at the left and the templates are in two columns.
   - **Create Resume with AI** (`#/dashboard/create-ai-resume/`) – Basic / Personalized tabs (Personalized adds the job-post field with a 0/6000 counter), name, job title, language, Generate with AI (purple gradient) → building screen → editor. Generate stays disabled until the name and job title are filled (in Personalized, the job post too).
   - **Generate work experience** (`#/ai/resume/` and `#/ai/resume/experience/`) and **Generate resume objective** (`#/ai/resume/general/`) – job title (+ company), language, Tone of Voice and Form chips (the chosen one widens and shows its name; the recommended form carries a Recommended badge), Generate → building screen → editor.
   - **AI Cover Letter Writer** (`#/dashboard/create-ai-cover-from-job/` and `#/ai/cover/`) – Basic: job title, job post, Generate with AI. Personalized: step 1 (Next) → step 2 **Select your resume** (a row of resumes, Back, Generate with AI).
   - **Job Interview Questions** (`#/dashboard/create-interview/`) – Basic: job title, Seniority (Junior / Mid / Senior), Generate with AI. Personalized: adds the job ad with a Paste button and a 0/1000 counter, then step 2 Select your resume.
   - **Resignation letter** (`#/ai/resignation/`) and **LinkedIn import** (`#/dashboard/linkedin-import/`, Import → building screen → editor).
   - **Upload resume** (`#/dashboard/upload-resume/`) – Upload your resume → (invalid file: Please upload a valid file) → Uploading your resume… → Continue → building screen → editor. Prototype triggers: a file name containing `scan`, a format other than DOCX, ODT, HTML, RTF, TXT or PDF, or a file over 10 MB shows the error.
   - **Tailor resume** (`#/dashboard/tailor-resume/`) and **Tailor resume to this job** (`#/jobs/job/1234/tailor/`, a stand-in URL; opened by Tailor my resume on a job page) – recreated from Figma (Kickresume 3.0 › tailoring setup and Tailoring new loading - update). The job post as a URL ("No URL? Paste job description" switches to job title, company name and the job post with a 0/6000 counter; "Got URL? Paste it instead" switches back), or, from a job page, the job's card (logo, title, company, posted, location, type, remote). Tailoring level: Conservative, Balanced (picked first) or Turbo, each with its one-line description; arrow keys move between them, and on phones only the picked level shows its name. Tailor Now (disabled until the URL or the job post is filled in; ready straight away with a job from a job page) opens the tailoring screen: four steps, Creating a copy, Scanning resume & job post, Tailoring and Finishing up, each with its own looping picture (a copy slides out from behind the resume, a glowing line scans it, keywords fly in while its lines rearrange, the templates slide by) and a progress bar that fills while it runs (3 s each here, about 30 s in the product). It ends in **Done!** (Continue → editor) or, with More options › Result set to Failed or Error, **Something went wrong.** (Retry runs it again after an error and goes back to the form after a failure; × goes back to the form). Clicking beside the card, the spinner button or Escape while it runs shows "Please wait for tailoring to complete." The card's corners are 16px and it has no drop shadow, like the other route screens; on phones the Tailor Now button stays inside the card (the design lets it overhang by 5px).
   - **Wireframes** – every screen without a design is a wireframe in Wire (Kubo's wireframe kit) in shades of grey only, so it stands out from the recreated screens. Its note (what the screen is for, which flow it belongs to) isn't on the screen: it shows in the prototype chrome at the bottom, above the flow bar (beside the phone frame in the mobile view, in its own row on a phone). Wireframes: the two resume tools that ask something first (`#/dashboard/rewrite-resume/` a tone, `#/dashboard/summary-generator/` a job title; their button opens what they make in the editor), the sign-up wall for guests (`#/register/` inside Flow 2's guest runs), the cover letter and website template pickers (`#/dashboard/create-cover/`, `create-website`), the jobs pages (`#/jobs/`, the job matcher `#/jobs/matcher/`, a job `#/jobs/job/1234/` (its Tailor my resume opens Tailor resume with that job), a company `#/jobs/organization/kickresume/`, the Job Tracker `#/jobs/job-tracker/`), Career Map onboarding (`#/career-map/`), and the flows' shared stages: processing, the processing state and the destinations that aren't designed yet (cover letter, letters, interview questions, website). `#/guest-edit-start/` opens the editor straight away.
   - **Headlines** – every personalization, processing and processing-state screen in the flows has the onboarding screens' headline and subheadline above its card (the question it asks, or what is happening), so the card starts where the goal cards, method cards and the building screen's resume do and nothing jumps between screens. Recreated screens keep their own card titles: Create Resume with AI ("What job are you going for?"), Generate work experience / objective, the AI Cover Letter Writer and Job Interview Questions (step 2: "Which resume should we use?"), Resignation letter, Upload resume ("Let’s bring in your resume", then "Uploading your resume"), LinkedIn import, Tailor resume ("Which job is it for?" / "Tailor it to this job", "Tailoring your resume", then its result). The wireframes give their title to the headline: the resume tools ("How should we rewrite it?", "What role is the summary for?"), the template pickers, the job matcher, Career Map onboarding, the shared processing (the feature's verb, "This takes a few seconds.") and processing state (Completed, Failed or Error, following More options › Result) and the guests' sign-up wall. All the copy is in `HEADS`. On phones every headline and subheadline keeps room for two lines for the same reason.
   - **Tools with nothing to ask** – Analyse resume, Translate resume, Recruiter feedback, Career coach and Proofreading have no setup screen. With a CV, `#/dashboard/analyse-resume/` goes straight to processing (Checking your resume) and then the ATS checker, under the same URL; `#/dashboard/translate-resume/` opens the Proofread & Translate page and brings its Translation part into view (the CV and the language come from a landing page before sign-up); `#/dashboard/recruiter-feedback/` and `#/dashboard/career-coach/` open the Career Coach page and `#/dashboard/proofreading/` the Proofreading page, each keeping its own URL. Without a CV the method screen comes first, then the building screen (for Analyse resume, Analyzing your resume), then the page.
   - **CV rule** – group 5 tools (and step 2 of the personalized writers) need a resume: without a CV they first show the **method screen** (LinkedIn, AI, Upload, Paste text), then the building screen, and then the tool (or the writer's step 2, with what was typed in step 1 kept) instead of the editor. The URL stays the tool's while that happens. Tailor resume and the ATS check skip that building screen: the method screen hands straight over to Tailor resume's setup, whose Tailor Now then shows the building screen worded for the job, or straight to the ATS check's own building screen (**Creating your resume** with matching and tailoring steps, or **Analyzing your resume** with the ATS checks; it ends in "Your tailored resume is ready!" or "Your ATS check is ready!") and goes straight to the editor (Tailor) or the ATS checker, without the tailoring card or the processing state (unless More options › Result is Failed or Error in a flow). With a CV they work as before: the tailoring card, or Checking your resume and the ATS checker. A CV counts once it's uploaded in onboarding or made this way; For testing, each flow in More options › Flows has its own CV choice, and More options › URLs has one for the screens opened from there. Group 4 (Generate with AI) asks for one only in the writers' personalized step 2.
7. **Four flow templates** – every URL follows one of four flows (the flow board in Figma); the URL only fills in the feature's own screens (its personalization), the wording of the processing and the destination. More options › Flows shows the four with their stages (preparation grey, processing sand, destination green; the outlined stages are the ones the URL changes), a picker with every URL grouped like the URL checklist, the flow's own CV choice (No CV / Has a CV) beside Run, and that URL's steps in order (coloured like the stage they fill, and redrawn when the CV changes them). Each flow keeps its CV choice, because one choice for all four couldn't fit every flow; a URL that decides it itself (a guest, a CV from a landing page, Latest resume, Analyse resume, and the tools that open their page straight away) shows it fixed, "set by this URL". The Result at the top of the view applies to every run. A bar at the bottom shows the run grouped by stage (hollow dots are wireframes; on a phone it gets a row of its own above the version tag and More options); ‹ › and the dots jump between steps, and the screens' own buttons move it on too. Restart, or opening a screen that isn't one of the run's steps (from URLs or by URL), ends it.
   - **Flow 1 · Goal first** – Intent collection (the goal screen) → feature personalization → data processing → processing state → destination; *Skip personalization* goes to the dashboard. After sign-up. The goal picked decides the feature: Build (method screen → building screen → editor), Tailor (with a CV: Tailor resume → its own tailoring screen, which ends in Done! or Something went wrong instead of the shared processing state → the editor's tailored copy; without one: the method screen → Tailor resume → the building screen, Creating your resume → the editor), the ATS check (with a CV: Checking your resume → the ATS checker; without one: the method screen → Analyzing your resume → the ATS checker) and Find jobs (job matcher → matching → job matches). Picking another goal during the run switches the run to it.
   - **Flow 2 · Feature first** – personalization → processing → state → destination: a feature's own URL. Templates (`/dashboard/create-resume|cover|website/`: Select your template for a resume, a wireframe picker for the other two), Generate with AI (the recreated forms; resumes go through the building screen into the editor, letters and interview questions through a processing wireframe to their result), Upload resume (upload → uploading → building → editor), LinkedIn import, the resume tools that ask something first (Tailor resume, Rewrite resume, Summary generator → the editor), Analyse resume without a CV (method screen → Analyzing your resume → ATS checker), Tailor resume to a job (from a job page: job page → Tailor resume with the job → tailoring → editor), the job matcher, Career Map, and two guest runs that meet the sign-up wall before the result.
   - **Flow 3 · Input given before** – processing → state → destination: the input came earlier, like a CV on a landing page (sign up · CV attached → uploading → building → editor, or → checking → ATS checker), a CV already uploaded (Analyse resume: checking → ATS checker), or a template or sample already in the URL (`…/{template}`, `/dashboard/create/{sample}/`).
   - **Flow 4 · Straight to the app** – the destination only: Log in (→ dashboard), the dashboard, Translate resume (after a landing page that took the CV and the language → the Proofread & Translate page), Recruiter feedback and Career coach (→ Career Coach) and Proofreading (→ Proofreading) with a CV, the guest editor, a website template, Latest resume (with and without a CV), My Documents, the editor, the jobs pages and the Upgrade pages.
   - **Processing and its result** – one processing screen for every feature (the designed building screen for resumes, a wireframe for the rest) that moves on by itself. Its result is Completed, Failed or Error (More options › Result): Completed goes straight on to the destination; Failed shows the processing state with a way back to change the input; Error lets the person try again. The processing state wireframe can switch between the three too.
   - Results that would open a screen the prototype doesn't have (a cover letter, interview questions, a resignation letter) show a short "Writing…" and then a note.

Content column is 950px, method cards are 500px.

The side rail lists its everyday settings in flow order: goal-screen layout (grid/list), method-screen variant (A/B), desktop/mobile, restart. A setting that doesn't affect the current screen is dimmed; clicking it applies it and takes you to the screen it changes. Less-used settings sit in a small panel behind an almost invisible More options button in the bottom-right corner (just a very light grey flask icon), which shows a faint dot while variant C, the 3D look or a Failed or Error result is on. The panel has two views, picked at its top (the last one picked comes back): **Flows** – the Result every flow's processing ends with (Completed / Failed / Error), the four flows (each with its own CV choice), and the goal and method screens' look (Flat / 3D, variant C); **URLs** – a CV choice for the screens opened from there (No CV / Has a CV), then every route screen at its URL.

- Source: `src/prototype.html`
- Build (inlines brand fonts, logo and the images in `assets/`): `python3 build.py` → `dist/create-resume-prototype.html` (self-contained, open in any browser)
- Version: shown bottom left (v1.3, v1.3.1, …); small changes bump the patch number, bigger ones the minor, the major only on request (see `CLAUDE.md`).
- Deep links: `#a` / `#b` / `#c` open a variant (an old `#d` link opens B in the 3D look), add `-3d` or `-flat` for the look, add `-mobile` for the phone view and `-list` for the list layout, `#editor` / `#dashboard` jump straight to those screens. Route screens use their path: `#/dashboard/create-ai-resume/` (and `#/dashboard/` opens the dashboard). The URL follows the route while it's on screen.
